Afternoon of Angels ( JAST Just )

Released: November 1985 Media: PC88 (FD2-disc set)
Last updated 2018.08.08

-Impressions & Strategies-
A super famous work in the early days of computer games that was the basis for the genre of "beautiful girl games". Command-type adventure game. The story is about the main character, who is a high school student, tries to somehow seduce "Yumiko", the most beautiful girl in the academy who attends the same academy, and gets various information from the people around him and gets closer to her.

At the start of the game, you will be greeted with the message "Now, with a passionate heart, a romantic adventure for you! Come on, LET'S LOVE!!" The message appears, but the characters to be entered are only "Mari Okasu " or "Chichi Lick" and there is no piece of romance (laughs) In the version upgrade released a year later, it is now compatible with the company's hardware, Just Sound, and audio output is possible. There is also an MSX version, but the girls are monochromatic! In addition to being drawn in the 88th version, the CG was significantly reduced and the commands received were less than the 88th version.

As an aside, this software is now commonplace, but it was the first software to adopt the method of importing and coloring the original drawings with a scanner. The company that was released by JAST was originally a hardware manufacturer that made measuring machines, and also developed scanners and other equipment. Therefore, for the purpose of promoting the sale of its own scanners, it seems that it was this "Tennoon" that sold software that imported and colored the original drawings of girls with its own scanners.

It may be said that it is the root of a sad game. I think this is definitely the best story in the "Afternoon of Angels" series.

Afternoon of Angels IV ~Yuko~ ( JAST Just )

Released: 1991 Media: PC-98 (FD2-disc set)
Last updated 2018.08.08

-Overview & Impressions-
Here at Sophia Academy, a prestigious school, a strange incident occurred in which students from the girls' dormitory wandered around every night. The main character is a single teacher who has been assigned to the post for three months after being asked by his relative, the principal, to investigate the matter. The main character infiltrates the girls' dormitory with the guidance of Yuko, a student in the class, and heads to a suspicious warehouse... An adventure game with a story called.

Until the previous work, the story was centered on love, but this work has a suspenseful touch and is mainly about solving mysteries. If you do unnecessary H-scenes, the feature of this series that goes directly to the bat end is still alive and well, so you need to make full use of the save road to watch the H-scenes. In addition, this work also does not have mosaics, so you can enjoy the CG itself (although it was discontinued because of that...). The heroine Yuko-chan (especially in bloomers) is cute~. The scenario is quite forced, and the case develops and is solved before you know it, so there is no part that bothers you at all. I feel like it's quite unsatisfactory, but I guess it's like this in JAST (laughs)

There has been an evolution around the game system, and from this work, the mouse is finally available, and the operability is more comfortable than before. I would also like to welcome the inclusion of ON/OFF for BGM and dialogue sound effects (settings can be changed by pressing the shift key). However, the problem is that there are scenes where commands must be selected in a certain order (= permutation combination). This is very stressful because it cannot be inferred from the appearance at all. However, it seems that you can get out by changing the message speed setting, so if you think you are stuck, you can try changing the message speed. It's also a pity that it doesn't support HDDs.

-Detailed strategy-
Basically, it's a one-way story, but depending on how you answer the fortune teller's question at the beginning, the H-scene is skipped or there are characters who end up in a sleepover. The points to be careful about the strategy are as follows.

1) If the fortune teller's second question is "dark place", Terumi will humiliate her in a back alley. If you make it a "bright place", you will have sex at the hotel.

2) In order to see the sex scenes of "Kozue Hashimoto" and "Chie Utsumi", the fortune teller's questions 5 and 8 are a combination of "shy daughter" & "love" or "bright daughter" > "friendship", respectively.

3) In order to see the sex scene with the fast food girl "Nana Hirose", you can meet her at a fast food restaurant before your date with Riza if you set questions 5 and 8 of the fortune teller to "bright daughter" and "love", respectively. Therefore, it will be an exclusive relationship with "Kozue Hashimoto" and "Chie Utsumi", so it is necessary to replay it at least twice. In addition, since Nana Hirose and the Hashimoto & Utsumi pair have sex scenes later, it seems better to play Nana Hirose on the main route and use the Hashimoto & Utsumi pair for replay.

4) On the second date with "Risa Kudo", it becomes an ectic scene with Riza when he "plays pranks" in the movie theater. When she "watches a movie", the sex scene with Riza is skipped, but later there is a 4P scene in Azusa's room. Can be responded to by saving/loading.
