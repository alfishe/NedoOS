Calling libc from a bank
------------------------
  Call puts/printf/? directly ? one copy lives in root (.com).
  No wrappers per API. Requirement: root must reference that symbol
  so xlink puts it into multibank.com (e.g. puts("") in mb_init).
  check_addrs.py verifies ttyputs/printf/? addresses match com vs ovl.

  Hang cause previously: bank CALLed puts, but .com had never linked
  puts ? CALL landed on a different routine.
